4 days · 20–35 passengers · Heathrow

London & Windsor for groups

A private ground programme for overseas tour operators. Keep your own hotel booking and ask us to quote transfers, private coach travel, guides and any admissions you want included.

  1. Day 1 — Heathrow arrival. Meet your driver and transfer to your London hotel. The rest of the day depends on your flight and check-in time.
  2. Day 2 — London sightseeing. A guided introduction to Westminster and central London, with time for lunch and a stop or visit agreed with your group.
  3. Day 3 — Windsor excursion. A private coach trip with time in Windsor. Ask us to include castle admission or quote Oxford as an alternative.
  4. Day 4 — Heathrow departure. Hotel pickup and airport transfer timed to your flight.

Send your dates, passenger count, luggage and hotel details. Your quote will set out inclusions and any optional extras. Services are subject to availability and confirmation.
