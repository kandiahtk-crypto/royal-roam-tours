Booking Terms

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Product pages and booking confirmations state the supplier, price, inclusions, exclusions, meeting arrangements and cancellation rules that apply to each booking. Where a third-party booking platform or accommodation supplier fulfils a service, its disclosed terms also apply.

Traveller responsibilities

Travellers must provide accurate information, arrive on time, follow safety instructions and hold required travel documents.

Changes

Routes and timings may change because of traffic, access restrictions, weather, closures or other circumstances outside reasonable control.
